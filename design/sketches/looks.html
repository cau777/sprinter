Why does this fail to compile? I only read first after the push.

Because first is a shared borrow of v that is still alive when push needs a mutable one. Pushing may reallocate the vector and leave first pointing at freed memory.

rustlet first = &v[0];
v.push(4);   // error[E0502]: cannot borrow `v` as mutable
println!("{first}");

Copy the value out instead: let first = v[0];
